Deepfake detection from camera noise

Every camera sensor leaves a unique fingerprint (PRNU) in the photos it takes. AI-generated images don’t have one. I built a lightweight CNN that uses this noise, plus high-pass filtering, to flag synthetic images, and wrote the research paper covering the method, experiments and results.
